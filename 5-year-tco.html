Warehouse management system 5-year TCO

Two worked examples, line by line. Fishbowl-tier SMB (single DC, 25 users, 15K orders/mo) sits at ~$140K all-in over 5 years. Manhattan-tier mid-market (3 DCs, 200 users, 250K orders/mo) sits at ~$3.5M-$4.5M before automation.

[SKU-C / SENSITIVITIES]

What blows the model up past year 2

  • User growth: +25% headcount at year 3 adds 25% to license + maintenance.
  • Automation upgrade (RF to GTP): adds $200K-$800K in WES configuration alone.
  • M&A: every new acquired DC adds $300K-$1.2M in implementation extension.
  • Tier-jump on per-order pricing: +$50K-$200K/yr per band crossed (model estimate).
[SKU-FAQ]

Buyer questions

Why are these two tiers, not a midpoint?

Because the vendor pricing is bimodal. A midpoint number would mislead both buyers. The two tiers represent the realistic price floors and ceilings for each segment.

Are the Manhattan-tier numbers tier-1 or mid-market?

Mid-market. Tier-1 retailer deployments at 25+ DCs land at $10M-$30M+ before automation.
