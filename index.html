[SKU-04 / FAQ]

What buyers actually ask

What does a warehouse management system cost in 2026?

Cloud SMB WMS starts at $229/mo (Fishbowl Inventory published). Mid-market SaaS WMS is quote-only; our TCO model sizes it at about $59-$160 per named user per month plus a platform fee. Tier-1 (Manhattan, Blue Yonder) is quote-only with implementation in the $250K to $2M+ band, benchmarked to Panorama Consulting's ERP-implementation data.

Which WMS vendors publish a price?

Fishbowl is the one vendor in our ledger that publishes a starting price: Fishbowl Inventory from $229/mo (billed annually) and Fishbowl Advanced Warehouse from $595/mo. The other 14 are quote-only. ShipHero used to publish a brand-tier software price but has removed its public pricing page and now routes to a demo, so we treat it as quote-only.

What is the average WMS implementation cost?

Benchmarked to Panorama Consulting's ERP-implementation data, mid-market WMS implementation runs $250K to $2M. The license-to-implementation ratio runs 1:1.5 for SMB cloud, 1:2 to 1:3 for mid-market, and 1:3+ for tier-1 enterprise. Conference-room-pilot phase alone is usually $40K to $120K.

Per-user, per-order, or perpetual: which model is cheapest?

Below ~30 named users and under ~10K monthly orders, per-order (Logiwa, ShipHero) and SMB SaaS (Fishbowl Inventory) win. Above ~50 users, per-user SaaS pulls ahead. Perpetual + 18-22%/yr maintenance only beats SaaS past year 5 with stable user counts.

What line items will the vendor AE skip in the demo?

RF guns ($1,500-$3,200 each), label printers ($800-$2,400), WiFi survey ($4K-$12K), EDI mapping ($800-$2,500 per trading partner), sandbox refresh, premier support, and change orders. We list every hidden item on /hidden-costs.
