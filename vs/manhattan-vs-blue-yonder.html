Manhattan vs Blue Yonder

Both are tier-1 quote-only. Manhattan Active WM is the cloud-native rewrite leading on store fulfillment and the Active platform. Blue Yonder Luminate WM is the JDA-heritage microservices rebuild leading on bundle breadth (WMS + TMS + Labor + DOM).

[SKU-A / ARCHITECTURE]

Cloud-native vs microservices

Manhattan Active WM is a ground-up cloud-native rewrite on a single platform with Active Maven AI orchestration bundled in 2025-26. Blue Yonder Luminate is microservices on the Snowflake-backed Luminate platform with the Cognitive Solutions overlay.[s-01][s-02]

[SKU-B / COST]

Implementation and subscription

Both quote-only; neither publishes a subscription rate. Third-party reference deals for tier-1 platforms cluster around $20K-$60K/mo, but nothing is published and we do not invent a midpoint. Implementation lands in a $250K-$2M mid-market band, benchmarked to Panorama Consulting's ERP-implementation data, and frequently exceeds $5M for tier-1 retailer deployments.[s-17]

[SKU-C / FIT]

Where each wins

  • Manhattan: omnichannel retail, store fulfillment, BOPIS, ship-from-store.
  • Blue Yonder: CPG distribution wanting WMS + TMS + Labor bundle.
  • Both: tier-1 with multi-DC global footprint.
[SKU-FAQ]

Buyer questions

Who is on the Gartner MQ Leaders quadrant?

Both have appeared in the Leaders quadrant in recent Gartner MQ for WMS. Specific positioning year by year is on the Gartner reviews page.

Implementation cost gap?

At a 1M-sqft DC, the implementation gap is usually within 10-20% and is dwarfed by scope decisions on store fulfillment, automation orchestration, and integration count.
