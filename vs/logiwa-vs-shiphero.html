Logiwa vs ShipHero

Two per-order cloud WMS for 3PL and DTC. Both are now quote-only on exact dollars: ShipHero has removed its public pricing page and quotes on demand, and Logiwa IO has never published exact per-order dollars. Both land in a similar band for 5K-200K monthly orders.

[SKU-A / PRICING]

Per-order economics

ShipHero no longer publishes a price on a public pricing page and quotes on demand[s-14]. Logiwa IO is also quote-only, and its per-order tiers land in a similar band[s-13]. Past ~100K orders, both push for re-tiering.

[SKU-B / INTEGRATIONS]

Marketplace coverage

Both have native Shopify, Amazon, and TikTok Shop. ShipHero has the broader brand-side integration list. Logiwa has the deeper multi-client 3PL ingest with per-client connector configuration.

[SKU-C / FIT]

Where each wins

  • ShipHero: single-brand DTC, optional fulfillment-network add-on.
  • Logiwa: multi-client 3PL with 5-20 brand clients, marketplace-heavy.
  • Either: small 3PL transitioning off spreadsheets or Extensiv-heritage legacy.
[SKU-FAQ]

Buyer questions

Onboarding fee and timeline?

Both run 6-12 week implementations with onboarding fees in the $5K-$25K range depending on integration scope. Onboarding is separate from monthly software.

Which has better marketplace coverage?

ShipHero has more brand-side connectors. Logiwa has more 3PL-side multi-client ingest depth. Test both with your actual marketplace mix before signing.
