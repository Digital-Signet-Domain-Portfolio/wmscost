[SKU-10 / FAQ]

ROI questions

What payback should I show the CFO?

SMB cloud WMS (Fishbowl Inventory tier) typically pays back in 6-14 months at >20K monthly orders. Mid-market (Korber, Infor, Tecsys) lands at 18-36 months. Tier-1 (Manhattan, Blue Yonder) lands at 30-60 months and is sold on labor + on-time-shipping uplift rather than software ROI alone.

Is the 25% productivity uplift realistic?

Yes for the paper-to-RF transition. Yes more conservatively (10-15%) for the RF-to-pick-to-light transition. No for the RF-to-goods-to-person transition without the matching hardware capex, which is a separate ROI model.
