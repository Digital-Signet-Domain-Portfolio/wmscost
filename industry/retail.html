[SKU-IND03 / RETAIL]

Retail WMS cost

Omnichannel retail at tier-1 means DC fulfillment + store fulfillment + BOPIS + ship-from-store. Manhattan, Blue Yonder, and Oracle dominate the category. Implementation runs into the seven and low eight figures at 25+ DCs.

[SKU-A / OMNICHANNEL]

Store fulfillment and DOM

Distributed Order Management (DOM) and store-fulfillment functionality price into the WMS contract as separate modules. Manhattan Active Store Fulfillment is a tier-1 module. Blue Yonder Luminate DOM is a separate platform layer. Oracle Retail Order Broker covers the same role.

[SKU-B / TCO BANDS]

By DC count

  • 5 DCs: $3M-$8M total 5-year TCO at mid-market WMS.
  • 25 DCs: $10M-$30M.
  • 100+ DCs: $40M+ at tier-1 Manhattan or Blue Yonder, before automation and store fulfillment.
[SKU-C / VENDORS]

Who shows up on the shortlist

Tier-1 retail shortlist is Manhattan Active WM, Blue Yonder Luminate, and Oracle WMS Cloud. SAP EWM appears for European retailers on S/4HANA. Korber appears for mid-market retail with HighJump heritage installs.

[SKU-FAQ]

Buyer questions

Best WMS for BOPIS and ship-from-store?

Manhattan Active Store Fulfillment is the category leader. Blue Yonder Luminate DOM covers similar scope as a separate platform. Both need DOM as well as WMS.

Mid-market retail at 5 DCs?

Korber, Tecsys, Infor are all credible at this scale. NetSuite WMS via SuiteSuccess for Retail is the budget path.
