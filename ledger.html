Ledger questions

Where is the CSV?

The CSV ships in v2 of this site. The on-page table is structured-data tagged as a Dataset so AI Overviews can lift it.

Why no Manhattan or Blue Yonder list price?

Both refuse to publish. Their AEs build the quote off warehouse volume, modules, integrations, and named users. Asking for a list price is a category error.
