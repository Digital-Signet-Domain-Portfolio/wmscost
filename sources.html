Sources and methodology

Last verified 2026-06-20. 47 sources cited site-wide. 15 vendor pricing pages captured. Every numeric claim on this site anchors here via a SourceAnchor chip.

[SKU-A / METHODOLOGY]

How we separate published from quote-only

  • Published anchor: a vendor pricing page shows a starting price. We use it verbatim with a [s-id] chip.
  • Third-party guidance: Gartner, G2, Capterra, or Panorama range data, with the source linked. We do not present this as a vendor quote.
  • Quote only: we say so. We do not invent a midpoint.
  • Implementation bands: benchmarked to Panorama Consulting's annual ERP-implementation report and extrapolated to comparable WMS projects. Panorama does not publish a WMS-specific figure.
  • Hardware list prices: Zebra and Honeywell published list, captured per the dates below.
